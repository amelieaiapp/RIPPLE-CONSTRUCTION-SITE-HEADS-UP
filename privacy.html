Privacy Policy

Ripple Heads-Up · Last updated: 6 August 2026

Short version: This app runs on your device. It does not collect personal data, does not track you, and does not send your programme files or notes to us or to any third party.

1. Who we are

Ripple Heads-Up is a site look-ahead tool for construction supervisors. Support contact: amelie.ai.support@gmail.com.

2. What the app does

You load an approved programme file (for example XER, Microsoft Project XML, or CSV) on your own device. The app shows a look-ahead, lets you hide activities, flag items for chase, and print or copy lists. A Quick Action Plan can be drawn by you without a programme file.

3. Data we collect

We collect no personal data.

The publisher does not operate a backend server for this app, does not require an account, and does not receive your files, notes, or usage analytics.

4. Data stored on your device only

The app may store information locally in your browser using technologies such as:

  • localStorage — for example a planner email you optionally save, or small preferences
  • IndexedDB — for example saved Quick Action Plans you choose to keep on that device

Programme files you open are processed in memory on your device. They are not uploaded to us. Clearing site data for this app in your browser removes local storage.

5. What we do not do

  • No advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No sale of data
  • No transmission of your programme or chase notes to the publisher

6. Microsoft Store and hosting

If you install the app from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft’s own terms and privacy statement apply to Store account, download, and update services. That is separate from this app’s own behaviour, which remains local on your device.

The web version may be hosted on GitHub Pages (or similar static hosting) solely to deliver the app files over HTTPS. Hosting providers may log standard server access data (for example IP address and time of request) under their own policies. The app itself still does not send your programme content to those hosts beyond the initial download of the app files.

7. Children

This app is intended for professional construction use. It is not directed at children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will appear on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

9. Contact

Privacy or support questions: amelie.ai.support@gmail.com.
